Privacy policy

Effective 29 October 2026

The short version: Cullery collects no data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet. Your photos go from your card to your Photos library on your iPhone and nowhere else. Nobody, including us, can see them.

This policy covers the Cullery app for iPhone and this website. Cullery is made by Michael Lameri (“we”).

What we collect

Nothing. Cullery does not send any information to us or to anyone else. It contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party code at all. It does not make network requests.

What stays on your iPhone

To do its job, Cullery keeps a few things in its own storage on your iPhone. None of it leaves the device.

Your marks
Picks, skips and stars for each shot, kept against the card they came from so they are still there when you plug the card back in.
Cards you have opened
A record of each card (its name, how many shots it holds and a fingerprint worked out from its file list) and, for up to 20 cards, the permission iOS gives Cullery to open that card or folder again without asking.
Import history
For each import: when it ran, how many shots were imported and checked, and which items in Photos came from which shots, so later imports can leave them out.
Previews
Thumbnails and previews of the card's shots, kept in a cache so the grid opens quickly. Files on their way to Photos are held briefly while they are checked, then handed to Photos.
Settings
Your choices, such as grid size and import options.

All of this is removed when you delete the app. Settings > Cards in the app also lets you forget the cards it remembers. If you back up your iPhone, the app's storage is included in that backup like any other app's, under Apple's terms.

Your card

Cullery only reads your card. It never changes, moves, renames or deletes anything on it, and it never writes marks or any other data to it. iOS asks for your permission before Cullery can read a card.

Your Photos library

Cullery asks iOS for access to Photos so it can add the shots you import, put them in an album, and check which shots are already in your library (by comparing file names and capture times) so it does not import them twice. It does this on your iPhone. It does not copy, upload or share anything from your library. You can change or turn off this access at any time in the Settings app under Privacy & Security > Photos.

Background imports

When you leave the app during an import, iOS shows its progress on the Lock Screen. This is shown by iOS itself on your device; no information is sent anywhere.

This website

This site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts, and loads nothing from other sites. GitHub, as the host, may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Email

If you email us for support, we use your email address and what you send only to reply to you, and we do not share it. Ask, and we will delete our copy of the conversation.

Children

Cullery collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal information from the app, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question or complaint about privacy, including under the Australian Privacy Act or the GDPR, email madebymike1@icloud.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If Cullery ever starts to collect any data, this page and the App Store privacy label will say so before that version is released.
