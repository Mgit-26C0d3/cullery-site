Support

Need a hand? Email madebymike1@icloud.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, camera, and what happened. A screenshot helps. You will usually hear back within two working days (Sydney time).

Getting started

What do I need?
An iPhone on iOS 26 or later and an SD card reader that works with your iPhone (USB-C, or Lightning on older iPhones). Cullery can also open any folder of photos in the Files app.
My card does not show up
Check the card is in the reader properly and the reader is pushed all the way in. If the card shows in the Files app but not in Cullery, open Settings, then Apps, then Cullery, and check card access is on. You can always tap Browse a folder on Cullery's home screen and choose the card in Files.
The card takes a while to open
Cullery reads the card's file list first, so a card with thousands of shots can take several seconds. The grid shows how far it has got, and shots appear as they are read.
Cullery asks for card access every time
Open the card once and allow access. Cullery remembers up to 20 cards and opens them by itself from then on. Settings > Cards in the app shows how many it remembers.

Marking shots

How do I pick and skip?
Tap a shot to see it full screen. Swipe up to pick it, down to skip it, or use the buttons. Tap a star to rate it. In the grid, tap Select and drag across shots to mark several at once.
What happens to skipped shots?
Nothing. Skipping only leaves a shot out of the import. It stays on the card and you can pick it later.
Where are my marks kept?
In the app on your iPhone, against that card. Plug the card in again and they are still there. Cullery never writes anything to the card.

Importing

Where do the shots go?
Into Apple Photos, in a new album named after the days you shot, unless you choose an existing album or none. You can rename the files on the way in.
What happens to RAW + JPEG pairs?
Cullery treats a pair as one shot. When you import, choose to keep both (Photos shows the JPEG and keeps the RAW with it), RAW only or JPEG only.
Can I leave the app while it imports?
Yes. The import keeps going in the background and iOS shows its progress on the Lock Screen. Keep the card reader plugged in until it finishes.
When is it safe to format my card?
When the import shows in Recent imports on the home screen with All verified. Every file was then read three times, from the card, from the copy and from the card again, and matched before it went into Photos. Format the card in your camera, not on your iPhone.
Some shots say they are already in Photos
Cullery recognises shots that are already in your library, however they got there, and leaves them out so you do not get doubles.
Some files on my card are not shown
Cullery shows RAW files iOS can read, JPEG, HEIF, and MP4 and MOV video. The grid notes any other camera files it found that Photos cannot take, so you know they are there.
Do my stars go into Photos?
On iOS 27 and later, yes. iOS 26 has no way for apps to set ratings in Photos, so there stars stay in Cullery.

Privacy

Cullery collects no data and never goes online. Read the privacy policy.
